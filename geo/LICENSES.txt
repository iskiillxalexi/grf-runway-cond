Map data used by the SIGMET globe and the Timezones globe
- world-50m.json, land-110m.json: Natural Earth 1:50m and 1:110m (public domain), as TopoJSON by world-atlas 2.0.2 (ISC license, Mike Bostock).
- fir.json: FIR outlines from the VATSpy Data Project (github.com/vatsimnetwork/vatspy-data-project), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0; simplified (top-level FIRs only, about 1 km). Approximate: for context only.
- The globe is drawn with d3-geo 3.1.1 and topojson-client 3.1.0 (ISC license).

Data used by Timezones
- tz-zones.json: time zone areas from timezone-boundary-builder, release 2026f (github.com/evansiroky/timezone-boundary-builder), the "with oceans, same since now" set. The data is made from OpenStreetMap: (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0, opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl. Changed here: simplified (about 4 km) and stored as TopoJSON. Approximate near a zone border.
- tz-places.json: airports from OurAirports (ourairports.com/data, public domain), each with its time zone looked up in the full timezone-boundary-builder data (via geo-tz); country names from OurAirports; the zones of each country from the IANA time zone database 2026f (zone.tab, public domain).
- us-states.json: the 50 states of the USA and the District of Columbia, from the U.S. Census Bureau's cartographic boundary files, 2017 edition (census.gov, public domain), as TopoJSON by us-atlas 3.0.1 (ISC license, Mike Bostock). Added here: where each name is written, and which time zone areas lie in each state (worked out from tz-zones.json; a zone covering under 1 % of a state is left out). The states' outlines and the countries' are drawn from different sources and differ a little along shores and lakes.
- No time zone rules are stored with the app: offsets and clock changes are calculated by the device's own time zone database.

Data used by Route
- rt-points.json: the significant points of the "Free Route Airspace (FRA) points list for the ECAC and neighbouring States", (c) EUROCONTROL (eurocontrol.int/publication/free-route-airspace-fra-points-list-ecac-area), one list per AIRAC cycle; here cycles 2611 and 2612 together. Used: name, position, type and location indicators. EUROCONTROL publishes the list for information; it is not a navigation database and may be incomplete. Navaids (VOR, DME, NDB, TACAN) from OurAirports (ourairports.com/data, public domain). Airports: tz-places.json, above.
- rt-places.json and rt-features.json: towns, capitals, countries, mountains, capes, mountain ranges, islands, deserts, seas, lakes and rivers from Natural Earth 5 (naturalearthdata.com, public domain), with the names in English and Swedish that Natural Earth carries. Natural Earth has no Swedish names for rivers and some of its river names have lost their accented letters: for about seventy rivers the name is set right, and the Swedish name added, by hand (the table in the build script). Shapes simplified to about 5 km. People and heights are Natural Earth's figures and some years old. A few capitals corrected by hand (Pretoria, Yamoussoukro, Naypyidaw, Dodoma, Porto-Novo, Gitega, Astana).
- rt-info.json: a few lines about each place of the PA notes: the opening of its article in the English Wikipedia (en.wikipedia.org), and in the Swedish one (sv.wikipedia.org) where people have written it, shortened to its first sentences, with remarks in brackets on pronunciation and other languages left out. The text is by Wikipedia's contributors and is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0); each text is shown with the title of its article and a link to it, where its authors are listed. The article of a place is found through the Wikidata id Natural Earth carries; for places without one it is looked up in Wikidata (wikidata.org, CC0) by name and position, so now and then a text can belong to another place of the same name.
