Terms of use

Your license key is valid. Read and accept the terms to open the app on this device.

The GRF Toolbox holds supplementary aids. Runway Cond applies the GRF flowcharts and the crosswind table to runway condition reports, to help you prepare the Flysmart inputs. SIGMET shows SIGMETs on a globe. Route draws the flight plan you paste, with what lies along it. Timezones shows UTC and local times.

It never replaces the Operations Manual, the Performance Manual, the RCAM or the approved performance software, and it is not an approved operational tool. Where they differ, the manuals apply.

Results can be wrong, for example when a report is read incorrectly, a value is missing, or a condition is not covered. Check every input and every result against the report and the approved documentation before use.

SIGMET shows SIGMETs from public sources (NOAA Aviation Weather Center, MET Norway) and from text or photos you enter. A SIGMET can be missing, late, read wrongly or drawn in the wrong place, and the FIR outlines are approximate. The SIGMET text and the official briefing apply.

Route draws the flight plan you paste on a map. Its points come from EUROCONTROL's list of free route points and from OurAirports, which can be out of date or incomplete; airways, SIDs and STARs are drawn straight, and a point that is not found is passed straight by. Times are spread between the EET figures of the flight plan and are not a nav log. The SIGMETs it picks out are the ones the app has read, compared with the route as drawn and the levels as filed: it can miss one. Equal time points are worked out from the route as drawn and the one wind you give: a cross-check, not the figures of the flight plan. The forecast it shows along the route is worked out from NOAA's GFS forecast model. Its wind, temperature, turbulence, icing and convection are model guidance: they can be wrong, coarse or out of date, and they are not the SIGWX chart, a SIGMET or the weather in your flight plan. The charts it opens are LFV's, with their own date printed on them. PA notes (places, sides, distances, people, heights) are approximate and come from open map data; the short texts about places are taken from Wikipedia and can be wrong or out of date. Route is not for navigation and does not replace the briefing package.

Timezones calculates every time from this device's clock and the time zone data in its operating system, and draws the zones from a simplified map. A time can be wrong when the device's clock is wrong, when a country has changed its clock rules and the device has not been updated, or for a point tapped near a zone border. Check a time that matters (a report time, a pick-up, a slot, a curfew) against the official source.

The flight crew remains fully responsible for the runway condition assessment, the performance calculation, the weather briefing and the safe conduct of the flight.

When it is online, the app tells the issuer when this device last synced and last showed a result, with the app version and the device code. This is encrypted on the way. Nothing you enter is sent, except in feedback you choose to send.
