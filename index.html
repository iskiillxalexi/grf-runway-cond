Terms of use

Your license key is valid. Read and accept the terms to open the app on this device.

GRF Runway Cond is a supplementary aid. It applies the GRF flowcharts and the crosswind table to runway condition reports, to help you prepare the Flysmart inputs.

It never replaces the Operations Manual, the Performance Manual, the RCAM or the approved performance software, and it is not an approved operational tool. Where they differ, the manuals apply.

Results can be wrong, for example when a report is read incorrectly, a value is missing, or a condition is not covered. Check every input and every result against the report and the approved documentation before use.

The flight crew remains fully responsible for the runway condition assessment, the performance calculation and the safe conduct of the flight.
